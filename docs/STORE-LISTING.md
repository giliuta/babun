## English

**Name (30):** Babun CRM
**Subtitle (30):** CRM for service businesses
**Promotional text (170):** Calendar, clients, sites, invoices and SMS reminders in one app — for technicians and small service teams.

**Description (4000):**

Babun is a CRM for service businesses — air conditioning, repairs, cleaning, installations and any team that works at the client's place.

CALENDAR
• Book a job by tapping free time; day, week, month and list views
• Each team has its own calendar, hours and day labels
• Works offline — changes sync when the connection is back

CLIENTS AND SITES
• Client base with phones, addresses, tags and sources
• Sites (objects) with photos, notes and a map pin
• Import clients from your phone contacts

MONEY
• Payment on every job, debts, income and expenses
• Invoices, receipts and credit notes in PDF
• Accounts, transfers, VAT and profit by team

TEAM
• Invite partners and choose what each of them can see or change, block by block
• Change history: who changed what and when

SMS
• Reminders and confirmations to your clients from your sender name

Your data stays yours: export it any time, delete your account from the app.

## App Review Information (Apple) — заметка проверяющему

> Babun is a business CRM for service companies. The demo account below has a
> team with sample clients, appointments and invoices. There are no in-app
> purchases: plans and SMS balance are managed on our website, and the app
> contains no links or calls to action for payment. Phone contacts are read
> only when the user opens "Import from contacts" and only selected contacts
> are saved. Account deletion: Office → Sign-in and security → Delete account.

Demo account: почта и пароль — их вводит владелец в App Store Connect (в git не
хранятся).

## App Privacy (Apple) — ответы анкеты (заполнено 04.10)

Возрастной рейтинг: **4+** (все ответы «нет»). В условиях поэтому нет
минимального возраста — Apple требует, чтобы он совпадал с рейтингом.

Tracking: **No** (данные не используются для слежки). Для каждого типа ниже:
Linked to the user — **Yes**, Used for tracking — **No**, Purpose — **App
Functionality**.

| Раздел | Тип |
|---|---|
| Contact Info | Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Physical Address |
| Financial Info | Other Financial Info (доходы, расходы, долги бизнеса) |
| User Content | Emails or Text Messages (тексты SMS клиентам) |
| User Content | Photos or Videos, Other User Content |
| Contacts | Contacts (только выбранные при импорте) |
| Location | Precise Location (точка объекта от клиента) |
| Identifiers | User ID |
| Purchases | Purchase History |
| Diagnostics | Crash Data (Linked — **No**) |

## Data safety (Google) — те же данные

Собирается: имя, почта, телефон, адрес, фото, файлы, контакты, точное
местоположение, история покупок, отчёты о сбоях. Передаётся третьим лицам —
нет (подрядчики-обработчики не считаются). Шифрование при передаче — да.
Удаление по запросу — да (ссылка выше). Рекламы нет.
